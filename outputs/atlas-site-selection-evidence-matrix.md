# ATLAS evidence-based site-selection and readiness matrix

Generated from the common 8 U.S. + 8 international register and pinned readiness/RTC audits. This is an eligibility crosswalk, not a weighted city ranking.

**Frame:** 16 candidates (8 U.S.; 8 international). **Confirmatory enrollment:** 0/16. **Initial RTC draw:** 169/192 groups passed before replacement. **Coverage-conditioned quota:** 192/192 groups and 16/16 areas meet the matched 12-group floor after screening 34 additional groups.

## Shared selection and enrollment criteria

- **public_research_need** — A location-specific rationale and public evidence URL are recorded for candidate screening. Supports public research/management relevance only; it does not establish buyer demand or validate the linked evidence source.
- **balanced_candidate_frame** — Eight U.S. and eight international candidate AOIs use the same nominal 625 km² footprint and OPERA DSWx-S1 V1 metadata window. A balanced candidate frame is not a representative sample of either region.
- **archive_metadata_discovery** — Report complete CMR metadata-hit and distinct-date counts for the same product/window; do not convert metadata to observations. Metadata discovers possible acquisition dates; it says nothing about valid pixels, floodwater, or event matching.
- **raster_availability_diagnostic** — Report the pinned 12-group-per-area 2025 Sentinel-1 RTC finite/non-nodata VV sample and its 80% spatial-coverage floor. RTC is a separate availability diagnostic, not OPERA DSWx-S1 validation or event/baseline coverage.
- **event_discovery_leads** — Report news-derived event leads and metadata-date opportunities as discovery counts only. Groundsource dates do not establish official event occurrence, exact time, QA-valid imagery, or independent labels.
- **confirmatory_enrollment** — Require verified event-specific coverage, at least three QA-valid scenes in each event and baseline period, full-AOI and asset coverage review, independent licensed labels, probability sampling, and the predeclared precision gate. No area currently satisfies the confirmatory gate; all remain candidates or exploratory leads.

## Interpretation

The candidate frame is supported as a broad, evidence-backed research screen, not as a completed comparable study. Public management evidence does not establish product demand. CMR metadata and Groundsource leads are discovery signals; RTC sample coverage is an auxiliary availability check. Reference-lead counts combine heterogeneous data sources and do not establish independent validation. No candidate passes the confirmatory enrollment gate, so the 8+8 comparative study is not yet empirically complete.
